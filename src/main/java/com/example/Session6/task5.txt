Transactional Emails

Sent because of a user's action.
Zomato: Order confirmation email after placing an order.
Flipkart: Order/shipping confirmation email after buying a product.

Marketing Emails

Sent to promote products, offers, or services.
Zomato: Discount or food offer email.
Flipkart: Festival sale or product offer email.


JavaMail Difference
Transactional: JavaMail sends the email immediately when an event happens, like placing an order.
Marketing: JavaMail may send emails in bulk using a scheduled job or a list of customers.
Transactional emails usually contain personal/order details.
Marketing emails mainly contain offers, discounts, and promotions.
